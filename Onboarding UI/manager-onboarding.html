Manager agreement · Adverse India

Manager agreement · Prepared for Sita Rao

This agreement is made on 27 November 2026 between Adverse India ("the Company") and Sita Rao ("the Manager").

1. Role

The Manager works as Account manager: plans and assigns the work of the clients given to them in Adverse, reviews it before it goes to the Company, keeps to the agreed quality and dates, and follows the Company's reasonable instructions.

2. Pay

The Manager is paid for the work they manage at the per-service rates agreed with the Company. Advances and payouts are recorded in Adverse and a statement is shared for each one.

3. Clients and pricing

The Manager does not agree prices, discounts or new work with clients; client pricing and billing stay with the Company.

4. Probation and notice

The first 3 months are probation. After that, either side gives 30 days' notice to end this agreement.

5. Confidentiality

The Manager keeps the Company's and its clients' information, footage, prices and plans private during and after this agreement, and does not share client material outside the Company.

6. Work belongs to the Company

Everything the Manager or their team makes for the Company or its clients belongs to the Company.

7. Clients and team

For 12 months after leaving, the Manager does not take on the Company's clients directly or hire away its team members.

8. Conduct

The Manager treats the team and clients with respect and follows the Company's policies.

9. Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of India.

By accepting online, the Manager agrees to these terms.
